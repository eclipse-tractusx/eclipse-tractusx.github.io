A cohort-based support program for projects, developers and organizations. It systematically lowers the technical and organizational barriers to entry and sustainably increases active participation in the open-source community.

Join an open meetingStart contributing

What the Accelerator offers

The Accelerator combines proven approaches from startup acceleration with the requirements of industrial open-source ecosystems.

  • Structured onboarding and qualification of new projects and developers

  • Test and development infrastructure

  • Targeted mentoring by experienced community members

  • Further development of governance, release and quality processes

  • Sustainable community and exchange formats

Who it is for

  • Projects

    Open-source projects that want to join Eclipse Tractus-X or grow inside it, with guidance on qualification, releases and quality.

  • Developers

    Developers who want to make their first contribution or take on more responsibility, with mentoring from experienced community members.

  • Organizations

    Companies and institutions that want to adopt the dataspace technology and actively participate in the open-source community.
