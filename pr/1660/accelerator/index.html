What the Accelerator is

Strengthening the community around Eclipse Tractus-X

Eclipse Tractus-X builds open-source dataspace technology together with an international community. The Tractus-X Accelerator supports that community: it works on financing models, better documentation and processes, and a stronger worldwide community, and it opens Eclipse Tractus-X to dataspace initiatives beyond Catena-X.

It defines the strategy, organizes events that bring people together more often and coordinates improvements to the software across projects. It runs 2026 – 2029 and is supported by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE).

Not a software development project. The Accelerator does not build Tractus-X itself. The software stays in the hands of the open-source projects, contributors and committers; the Accelerator coordinates, connects and enables them.

What we work on

Six goals for the community

Together, they make Eclipse Tractus-X easier to fund, to join and to build on.

  1. 01

    Financing models

    Work with partners and the community on ways to fund the open-source work around Eclipse Tractus-X in the long term.

  2. 02

    Better documentation and processes

    Improve the quality of documentation, governance, release and quality processes, so that joining and contributing takes less effort.

  3. 03

    A stronger worldwide community

    Grow and connect contributors, projects and organizations across countries and industries.

  4. 04

    Welcoming more dataspace initiatives

    Open Eclipse Tractus-X to dataspace initiatives beyond Catena-X and help them onboard and reuse what the community has built.

  5. 05

    Strategy and coordinated improvements

    Define where the community goes next and coordinate improvements to the software across projects. The Accelerator coordinates; the community and its committers build.

  6. 06

    Events that bring people together

    Hackathons, thinkathons and the flagship Community Days, organized with intent so the community meets more often.

How to join

Your way into the community

You don't need an invitation to start. These steps use the community's open channels.

  1. 01

    Meet the community

    Join the Community Office Hour, every Friday from 10:05 to 11:00 (Europe/Berlin). It is open to everyone, and the right place to ask about the Accelerator.

    Open meetings →
  2. 02

    Get set up

    Follow the getting started guide to find your way around the repositories, the tooling and the way the community works.

    Getting started →
  3. 03

    Make your first contribution

    Pick an issue, open a pull request and get feedback from committers. The contribution guide explains each step.

    How to contribute →
  4. 04

    Build with us at an event

    Hackathons, thinkathons and the Community Days bring newcomers and experienced members together to work on real challenges.

    See the events →

Events

Meet, think and build together

Intentional events bring the community together more often, in three formats.

  • Hackathons

    Hands-on working days on real challenges of the Tractus-X software.

  • Thinkathons

    Focused sessions to think through strategy, architecture and open questions together.

  • Community Days

    The flagship event of the community: talks, workshops and exchange over two days.
