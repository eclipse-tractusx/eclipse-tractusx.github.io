Hi Everyone 👋,

After the great energy of our **first international Open Source Hackathon** in Bilbao this February, it is time for round two: we are happy to invite you to the **Second International Eclipse Tractus-X Hackathon**, taking place on **November 6, 2026 at ARENA2036 e.V. (Pfaffenwaldring 19, 70569 Stuttgart, Germany)**! 🚀

## Event Details

- **💸 Cost:** FREE 🆓
- **📅 Date:** 6th of November 2026
- **📍 Location:** ARENA2036 e.V., Pfaffenwaldring 19, 70569 Stuttgart, Germany
  *[ARENA2036 on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=ARENA2036+Pfaffenwaldring+19+70569+Stuttgart)*
- **⏰ Duration:** Full Day Event

## ✉️ Interested? Let Us Know

There is no registration form this time — **if you are interested in joining, simply send an email to [mathias.moser@catena-x.net](mailto:mathias.moser@catena-x.net?subject=Eclipse%20Tractus-X%20Hackathon%20II%20%E2%80%94%20November%206%2C%202026)** with your name, your organization, and a short note on what you would like to work on. We will get back to you with all further details. 📬

:::tip[Please reserve the date!]
Places are limited and the hackathon is a hands-on developer event — so if you are planning to come, let us know as early as possible so we can plan the challenges and the room accordingly.
:::

### ‼️ IMPORTANT

- **Please reserve this date if you're willing to come!** 🗓️
- This is a **"WORKING/DEVELOPING" hands-on event** — send this to your developers!
- It is meant to be **technical** and we will be solving challenges together
- This is **NOT a "business oriented"** event
- **SO BRING YOUR LAPTOPS!** 💻 And lots of motivation to code and learn! You can use your favorite IDE (we recommend visual studio code!)

## 🧩 Hackathon Challenges

The concrete challenges and the detailed agenda will be announced closer to the event. As in the first edition, they will be chosen to support our community goals for the upcoming Tractus-X release — so what you build at the hackathon lands directly in the open-source components the whole ecosystem uses.

Do you have a challenge in mind — a component you want to push forward, an integration you want to see in the [Umbrella](https://github.com/eclipse-tractusx/tractus-x-umbrella), or a KIT you want to bring to life? **Tell us in your email!** The best challenges come from the community itself.

## 🔙 Looking Back — Hackathon I in Bilbao

![First International Eclipse Tractus-X Hackathon — Bilbao, February 2026](@site/static/img/events/hackathon-spain.jpeg)

In February 2026, the Eclipse Tractus-X community met for its **[first international hackathon](/blog/first-international-hackathon)** in Bilbao, Spain — a full day of coding on the Tractus-X Identity Hub Wallet and the Industry Core Hub, hosted by our Project Lead Mikel Garcia and LKS Next. It showed what a room full of motivated developers can achieve in a single day, and it is exactly that spirit we want to bring to ARENA2036 in November.

## 📍 One Month Before the Community Days

The hackathon takes place just a few weeks before the **[Seventh Eclipse Tractus-X Community Days](/blog/community-days-12-2026)** (December 3–4, 2026), also at ARENA2036. What we build together in November is a perfect warm-up — and a great story to share with the whole community in December.
