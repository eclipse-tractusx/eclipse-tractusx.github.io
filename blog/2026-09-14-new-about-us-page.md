Hi Everyone 👋,

Who is behind Eclipse Tractus-X? How do so many organizations build one open-source project together? And where is the project heading? Until now, the answers were spread across blog posts, meeting minutes and presentations. That changes today: we are happy to present our **brand-new [About Us page](/AboutUs)**! 🎉

**Community Built. Open to everyone.** — that is the message at the heart of the page. Eclipse Tractus-X brings together contributors from different organizations, open-source projects, industry initiatives and independent companies, and the new page tells that story in one place.

<!-- truncate -->

## 🧭 What You Will Find

### 🌐 Our Ecosystem

Industry initiatives, open-source projects, companies and individual developers all play a different role in the project. A new diagram shows how these groups connect and how their knowledge flows into shared building blocks.

### 🤝 Organizations Engaged in Eclipse Tractus-X

A logo carousel introduces organizations whose teams help build and support the project, from industry associations and research institutes to OEMs, suppliers and service providers.

### 📜 Our History

A timeline takes you from the **initial contribution by the Catena-X consortia in 2023**, through the **KIT 2.0 architecture**, the extension **beyond automotive** and the **cross-industry collaboration with Factory-X and the Eclipse Dataspace Working Group**, all the way to what is ahead.

### 🚀 The Tractus-X Accelerator (2026 – 2029)

The newest milestone on the timeline is the **Tractus-X Accelerator**, a cohort-based support program for projects, developers and organizations, supported by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE). It lowers the technical and organizational barriers to entry and focuses on:

- Structured onboarding and qualification of new projects and developers
- Test and development infrastructure
- Targeted mentoring by experienced community members
- Further development of governance, release and quality processes
- Sustainable community and exchange formats

More information is about to come soon!

Our upcoming **[Second International Eclipse Tractus-X Hackathon](/blog/hackathon-ii-11-2026)** on November 6, 2026 at ARENA2036 is organized as part of the Accelerator.

### 🛠️ How We Work and What We Create

The page also explains how we get **from shared needs to shared solutions**, gives an overview of our **open-source software** and **KITs**, and shows how we collaborate under the **Eclipse Foundation** and together with the **Eclipse Dataspace Working Group**.
