## 🎯 The Mission: Re-architecting the Eclipse Tractus-X Portal

The main reason for this hackathon is the **re-architecture of the [Eclipse Tractus-X Portal](https://github.com/eclipse-tractusx/portal)**. The Portal is the front door of every data space built on Tractus-X technology — it is where companies get onboarded, register, and start participating. We want to make it:

- ✨ **Simpler** — a leaner architecture with fewer moving parts
- 🔧 **Easier to maintain** — so the community can keep it evolving without a steep learning curve
- ⚡ **More performant** — fast where it matters, from the first login to the last onboarding step
- 🚀 **Easier to use for data space onboardings** — so onboarding into a data space with Tractus-X tech becomes straightforward, for operators and participants alike

### 🧠 Design Thinking + 💻 Programming

This is not a "code first, think later" hackathon. We will start with **design thinking**: together we look at how onboardings work today, where the friction is, and what a simpler Portal architecture should look like. Then we switch into **programming mode** and start building the first pieces of that architecture — hands-on, in the open, in the Tractus-X repositories.

Whether you are an architect, a developer, a product person, or someone who has onboarded companies into a data space and knows the pain points first-hand — **your perspective is needed** at the table.

The detailed agenda and the concrete working tracks will be announced closer to the event. Do you have ideas, requirements, or war stories about the Portal and onboarding? **Tell us in your email!**
