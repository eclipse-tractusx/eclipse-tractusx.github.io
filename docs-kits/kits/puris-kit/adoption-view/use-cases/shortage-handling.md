PURIS supports a structured operational shortage management process whenever a potential supply disruption is identified.

Both customer and supplier continuously assess the current supply situation using operational information exchanged through PURIS. If a critical supply situation is detected, the involved parties evaluate the root cause and determine appropriate mitigation measures.

Depending on the situation, these measures may include production adjustments, delivery replanning, inventory prioritization, special logistics measures or coordinated mitigation activities.

As updated operational information becomes available, the supply situation is reassessed, and mitigation activities are adjusted accordingly. This iterative process enables both parties to maintain a common understanding of the supply situation and coordinate shortage management activities until supply capability has been stabilized.

The operational shortage management process typically includes the following steps:

- Assess material supply situation
- Identify critical supply situations
- Perform root cause analysis
- Determine mitigation options
- Exchange operational PURIS information
- Initiate mitigation measures
- Update operational information
- Reassess the supply situation
- Continue coordinated shortage management until stabilization is achieved

The generic operational shortage management process is illustrated on the [Use Cases](use-cases.md) overview page.
