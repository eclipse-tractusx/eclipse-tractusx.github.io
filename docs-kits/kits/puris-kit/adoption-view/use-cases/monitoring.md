A key benefit of PURIS Monitoring is the reduction of the operational impact of shortages.

PURIS does not primarily reduce the probability of disruptions occurring. Instead, it enables earlier visibility of developing supply issues, shortens reaction times and expands the available mitigation options for supply chain partners.

As a result, organizations can reduce the potential business impact of disruption through earlier coordination, faster decision-making and more targeted mitigation measures.
